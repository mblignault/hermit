# Help text

For `help`, show this, shortened to what's useful. For `help <command>`, show only that row and a one-line example.

**Home Hunt: commands**
- `setup`: the first-run interview. It builds your profile, tests the listing sites, schedules the scout and builds your board.
- `scout`: check the sites now for new listings.
- `add <link>`: score one listing and put it on the board. A pasted description or a screenshot works too.
- `board`: open or rebuild your board.
- `asks`: every question to put to agents, grouped by listing.
- `verify`: check that every active listing is still on the market.
- `settings`: see or change your budget, areas, places you travel to, must-haves or weights. `settings calibrate` re-tunes the scoring from places you love or hate.
- `sources`: see the sites I check and how; `sources test` re-checks them; `sources add <site>`.
- `schedule`: see or change when the scout runs (`schedule daily 07:00`, `schedule off`).
- `status`: a quick summary.
- `export` / `export csv`: your data.
- `reset`: start over. I back everything up first.

**How scores work, in short:** each listing gets 0–100 from your own weights: location (your areas and your real trips), price, pets, garden, entertaining, layout, type, what's within walking distance, timing and features. A deal-breaker, like no pets for your dogs or the wrong side of an area you only half-like, makes it "no fit", whatever else is great. Unknowns score in the middle and turn into questions for the agent. 85+ is strong, 72+ good, 58+ maybe.

**Board tips:** drag cards between columns. Open a card to fix facts, and the score updates. Stars are each person's own rating after a viewing, kept separate from the fit score. To link two listings of the same place, hold one card over the other.
