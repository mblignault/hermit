# Help text

For `help`, show this, shortened to what's useful. For `help <command>`, show only that line and a one-line example. People can also just ask in plain words.

**Hermit: what you can say**

Everyday
- `scout`: check the sites now for new listings.
- `add` + a link: score one listing and put it on the board. A pasted description or a screenshot works too.
- `questions`: everything to ask agents, grouped by listing, ready to paste.
- `board`: open or rebuild your board.
- `status`: a quick summary, including when the last scout ran.

Tuning
- `settings`: see or change your budget, dates, areas, the places you travel to, or must-haves.
- `settings calibrate`: star a few places you like and one you don't, and I'll tune the scoring to match your gut.
- `sources`: the sites I check and how; `sources test` re-checks them; `sources add <site>`.
- `schedule`: when the scout runs (`schedule daily 07:00`, `schedule weekdays 07:00`, `schedule off`).

Admin
- `verify`: re-check that every listing on your board is still on the market.
- `export` / `export csv`: your data.
- `reset`: start over. I stop the schedule and back everything up first.

**How scores work:** each listing gets 0–100 from your own priorities: location (your areas and your real trips), price, pets, garden, entertaining, layout, type, what's within walking distance, timing and features. A deal-breaker, like no pets for your dogs or the wrong side of an area you only half-like, makes it "no fit", however nice the rest is. Anything the listing doesn't say scores in the middle and becomes a question for the agent. 85+ is strong, 72+ good, 58+ maybe.

**Board tips:** drag cards between columns. Open a card to fix facts, and the score updates. Stars are each person's own rating after a viewing ("Alex 4★ · Sam 2★" on the card, amber when you disagree), separate from the fit score. To link two listings of the same place, hold one card over the other.
